Our story

Near the Helford, where the shapes come from.

Kota Sound is a small, family-run company based near the shores of the Helford River in Cornwall. We draw inspiration from the natural world around us and bring its forms into the technology we make.

“With nothing to do but wait for my glue-up to dry, I began to play with these bits of wood, wrapping them around a tiny speaker to see if I could make it sound better or louder. One of the strips I had made into a hoop sprang open to form a spiral. I recognised the shape straight away as a Fibonacci spiral.”

Midbar Dahan, founder and inventor

The first prototype

He put two sides on that spiral and made it into the first version of what became the Kota range. Within a few weeks there were more than thirty prototypes: double spirals, ported enclosures, tight curves and loose ones, big and small. In the end he came back to the shape he had started with, and began the research and development that followed.

Midbar has been an inventor from a young age, redesigning everyday things to make them work better. His patent for the spiral loudspeaker housing, GB2557980B, was granted in 2019.

Family

Fibonacci Technology

Kota Sound grew out of Fibonacci Technology, founded by Midbar Dahan to explore technology based on the designs of nature. The Kota speakers are its first product line.
