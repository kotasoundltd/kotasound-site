Biomimetics

Learning from nature's designs

Biomimetics is the growing field of observing how nature solves problems, and carrying those solutions into the things we design and make.

To comprehend and copy nature.

Nature has been running the longest research programme there is. Over hundreds of millions of years it has settled on forms that move water, carry load, store energy and spread sound and light with almost nothing wasted: the chambers of a shell, the curve of a wing, the turn of a vortex.

Why we work this way

Our passion for biomimetic design drives everything we make. Where much of the industry starts from a box and corrects it afterwards, we start from a form nature has already proven, and build the technology around it.

The spiral in our speaker

Studying the Fibonacci spiral, a pattern that occurs throughout nature, gave us a new way to think about sound. Our patented design shapes the speaker's chamber around it.

Beyond sound

We are exploring principles that reach far beyond audio.
