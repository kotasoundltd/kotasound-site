The idea

A shape found throughout nature

To comprehend and copy nature, we draw inspiration from our surroundings, incorporating the perfection of nature's designs into our technology.

Utilising shapes, mathematics and materials, we bring you a range of products that is in harmony with nature.

Our speaker breaks free from the generic boxes seen across the rest of the industry. Its curved, spiralling form follows a shape nature has refined over millions of years, and brings a quiet beauty to any space it sits in.

Inspired by nature

The same spiral you find in shells, sunflowers and the curl of a fern, shaped into a speaker.

Designed in Cornwall

An idea born in a Cornish workshop, near the shores of the Helford River, surrounded by nature and its design.

Made to last

Machined from high-grade extruded aluminium, built to last a lifetime and still look almost as it did the day it was made. Every part is designed to be repaired, replaced and maintained, should it ever need it.

For everyone who listens to music

Two ways to listen

For PleasureThe P4, voiced with our signature sound for the music you love.Tap to read more

At home on a shelf, out in the garden, at the gathering or on the road, the P4 is tuned out of the box with the Kota signature sound: rich, open and alive, made for listening rather than analysing.

It comes with its own edition of the Kota app, which sets the pair to your room in a few taps using the microphone in the box. And if you ever want to go further, you can step up to the Studio Pro app at any time.

Explore the P4

For ProfessionalsThe Studio Hub P4, set up as a studio monitor out of the box.Tap to read more

For the studio, the set, the location and the edit, the Studio Hub P4 arrives set up as a studio monitor, in a hard-wearing professional finish.

It comes with the Studio Pro edition of the Kota app: calibrate the pair to your room as a reference, measure it, and trust what you hear wherever the work takes you.
