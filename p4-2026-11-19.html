Studio Hub P4

A studio monitor you can carry.

Portable and battery powered, with a sealed spiral chamber machined from solid aluminium. One full-range driver, no crossover, and a shape that sits as happily on a desk as on a stage floor.

Many hats

One speaker, three ways to stand.

Flat

Desk monitor

Standing on its flat base it faces you at ear height as a nearfield monitor for mixing, editing or listening.

On its curve

Floor wedge

Rolled onto its curved face it tilts up at about 45 degrees, so it can sit on a stage or table and aim at the listener.

With the horn

Longer throw

Placed in its outer casing, the spiral continues into a wider mouth for larger rooms and the outdoors.

Field test · December 2021

distance 5 m – 80 mangles −45° to +45°coverage at 1 kHz, −6 dB: ≈ 90° large casing, ≈ 60° medium

Proven at scale

The same spiral, at venue size.

Before the P4, the spiral architecture was built as a 12-inch cabinet with interchangeable horn casings and measured outdoors along a line from 5 to 80 metres. The casing sets the width of the sound field, which is the point: sound that goes to the audience instead of the neighbours.
