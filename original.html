Unlock the Future of Sound with Biomimetic Design

Inspired by Fibonacci mathematics, the KotaSound P4 is designed to reduce unwanted resonance for a purer listening experience.

Our patented SpiroSound™ technology applies these fundamental principles of nature to the internal structure of the speaker, creating an acoustic environment that more closely follows nature.

This natural geometry manages sound waves inside the speaker, minimising internal reflections and unwanted resonance, for a cleaner, more precise sound.

Groundbreaking Audio Engineering

The external design of the P4 continues the spiral shape, guiding the sound as it leaves the speaker so that it spreads smoothly and evenly.

The aim is a sound that keeps its form and character as it travels, so music sounds full and rich even at lower volumes.

About Fibonacci Technology

At Fibonacci Technology, we strive to bridge the gap between theoretical concepts and practical engineering by drawing inspiration from nature's perfected designs.

This involves developing new manufacturing techniques and materials, leading to continuous learning and adaptation.

While our current focus is on audio technology, the principles we explore have potential applications in energy generation, materials science, architecture and medicine.

By working to “comprehend and copy nature”, in Viktor Schauberger's words, we aim to align with sustainable practice, minimising waste and making the best use of resources, just as nature does.
